## Ingredients

* 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, drained  
* 15 g fresh ginger, finely chopped  
* 1 tsp ground cumin  
* ¼ tsp asafoetida  
* 1 tsp rock salt  
* 180 g *maṇḍaka* dough  
* Sesame oil (or another neutral oil), for shallow-frying

## Method

1. Mash the drained chickpeas coarsely.  
2. Mix with the ginger, cumin, asafoetida, and salt.  
3. Divide the dough and filling into 8 portions. Enclose the filling and roll each into a round.  
4. Shallow-fry in sesame oil (or another neutral oil) over medium heat, 2–3 minutes per side, until browned in spots and cooked through.
