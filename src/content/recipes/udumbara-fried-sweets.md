## Ingredients

* 200 g *maṇḍaka* dough  
* 150 g cooled *kīsāra*  
* Neutral oil, for frying

## Method

1. Divide the dough and *kīsāra* each into 16 portions.  
2. Flatten a piece of dough, place the filling in the centre, and pinch the dough closed around it.  
3. Roll gently into a small round or fig shape about 3 cm across.  
4. Shallow-fry in batches over medium heat, turning as needed, until golden all over, 3–4 minutes.  
5. Drain and serve warm.
