This is a meat salad. It's unclear what the *kesarāmla* or the *gṛñjana* are. The former, perhaps, is related to the citron, the latter some form of allium. I've used spring onions here.

## Ingredients

* 500 g cooked grilled lean meat: pork loin, lamb leg or loin, or goat
* 1 lemon, peeled — juice squeezed out and the pulp finely chopped
* 15 g fresh ginger, finely chopped
* 2 spring onions, finely chopped
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* ½ tsp ground black pepper
* 1 tsp rock salt
* 15 g ghee
* ¼ tsp asafoetida

## Method

1. Chop the meat by hand until the pieces are about the size of sesame seeds. Cut the ginger and spring onion to match.
2. Mix with the lemon pulp and juice, ginger, spring onion, cumin, pepper, and salt.
3. Heat the ghee until hot, stir in the asafoetida and let it bloom for a few seconds, then pour it over the meat. Toss and serve.

**Notes**

* The text calls for asafoetida twice, in two forms: powdered and mixed into the meat along with the rock salt (1477), and then again as smoke — *hiṅgu-dhūpa*, asafoetida incense — to perfume the finished dish (1478). I have not yet worked out how to practically achieve the latter effect in my kitchen.
