Meatballs stuffed with meat...

## Ingredients

For the filling

* 300 g of the [Roast-Meat Salad](/recipes/manasollasa/roast-meat-salad-with-citron-and-ginger/), made as in 1476–77

For the casing

* 500 g finely minced lamb, goat, or pork  
* ¼ tsp asafoetida dissolved in 30 ml water  
* 1½ tsp salt  
* 1 tsp ground cumin  
* ½ tsp ground black pepper

To cook

* Sesame oil or neutral oil, for frying

## Method

1. Work the mince by hand with the asafoetida-water, salt, cumin, and pepper until it is smooth, tacky, and holds together like a stiff dough.
2. Divide the paste into 10, and the filling into 10 portions of about 30 g. Flatten each piece of paste in a wet palm, put a portion of filling in the middle, close the paste over it, seal the seam, and pat it down even.
3. Shallow-fry over medium heat, in enough oil to come a third of the way up, turning every couple of minutes until browned all over and the casing reads 71°C — 10–12 minutes.
