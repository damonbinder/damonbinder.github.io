## Ingredients

* About 450 g of small fish like anchovies or sardines (I used 5 whole sardines)  
* 100 ml white wine  
* 30 ml olive oil  
* 30 ml fish sauce  
* ½ tsp dried oregano  
* ½ tsp dried rue  
* Pepper

## Method

1. Place the fish in a frying pan along with the wine, oil, fish sauce, oregano, and rue. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes, or until the fish are cooked through.   
2. Remove the fish from the frying pan, sprinkle with pepper, and serve with just a little of the cooking liquid.
