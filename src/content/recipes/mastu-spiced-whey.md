## Ingredients

* 250 ml fresh whey, drained from one batch of *śikhariṇī*  
* ½ tsp cumin seeds, crushed  
* 1 tsp ginger juice  
* 2 g rock salt (about ⅓ tsp)  
* A pinch of asafoetida

## Method

1. Whisk the cumin, ginger juice, rock salt, and asafoetida into the whey.  
2. Serve at room temperature or gently chilled.
