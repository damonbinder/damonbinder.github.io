translation: >-
  Of fish that carry scales, the scales are to be knocked off with care. If they are large they are to be made into pieces; if small, kept in their own form. One should cut off the head of the fish, and the tail of those fin-bearers. Having slit the belly, he should draw the entrails out of it. *Kahlāṃ*, *karoṣṭra*, *baḍiśa*s, *dagdhā*, *khavala*, together with the *cācuka*s, and likewise with the *pāṭhīna*s, the sheatfish, which carries no scales — of these, having separated the head, one should cook it. The fish are to be rubbed with oil and with salt, until they go slimy and the fishy smell is destroyed. Afterwards he should wash them with waters mixed with turmeric paste; having tied them up in a cloth and pressed them, he should let the gathered water run off. He should throw them into the *prapaka*s and pans already made known. When a little time has passed, the cook should take the pot off the fire and fumigate it. Or he should cook the washed fish pieces right through with tamarind sour; then he should scatter wheat flour over them on every side, throw them into heated oil, and take them out when they are tawny. He should finish them with powdered cardamom and pepper and with rock salt.

The oil-and-salt rub, turmeric rinse, and tamarind simmer didn't add much, so I'd recommend skipping them unless you want to be maximally authentic.

## Ingredients

* 500 g firm white fish fillets or steaks  
* 2 tsp salt, divided  
* *(Optional)* ½ tsp turmeric  
* *(Optional)* 30 g tamarind concentrate  
* 50 g wheat flour  
* Neutral oil, for the rub shallow-frying  
* ¼ tsp ground cardamom  
* ½ tsp ground black pepper

## Method

1. *(Optional)* Rub the fish with 15 ml oil and 1 tsp salt for 1–2 minutes. Rinse briefly in water mixed with turmeric, then pat completely dry.  
2. *(Optional)* Dilute the tamarind in 150 ml water. Simmer the fish in this liquid for 2–4 minutes, just until the exterior firms, then drain and cool.  
3. Dust every surface with wheat flour.  
4. Shallow-fry over medium-high heat, turning once, until browned and cooked through.  
5. Grind the cardamom, pepper, and remaining salt together and sprinkle over the hot fish.
