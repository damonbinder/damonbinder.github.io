  And taking the fatty portion of the *śuṇṭhaka*s that have been sweated, and having made fine *cakkalikā*s, shaped just like a palmyra leaf, he should throw the relishing *cakkalikā*s into *mathita* combined with sugar, mixed with cardamom, and perfumed with camphor.

## Ingredients

* 500 g cooked skinless roast pork belly, chilled
* 20 g ghee
* 20 g sugar
* ¼ tsp ground cardamom

## Method

1. Slice the chilled pork belly into pieces about 3 mm thick and 5–7 cm long.
2. Crisp the pork slices in the ghee over medium-high heat.
3. Off the heat, toss the pork with the sugar and cardamom, and serve.
