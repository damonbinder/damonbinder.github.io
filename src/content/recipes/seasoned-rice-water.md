It is not entirely clear what this is supposed to be, but my best guess is as a thin sauce to be eaten over rice.

## Ingredients

* 500 ml water from the second washing of rice  
* 20 g tamarind concentrate  
* 75 ml *takra*, buttermilk, or plain yoghurt
* 30 g sugar  
* ¼ tsp ground cardamom  
* 15 ml ginger juice  
* ¼ tsp asafoetida

## Method

1. Dry-roast the asafoetida in a small pan over low heat for about 30 seconds, until fragrant, then tip it out of the pan.  
2. Whisk the tamarind, sugar, cardamom, ginger juice, and asafoetida into the rice water.  
3. Whisk in the *takra* last.  
4. Serve cool, spooned over rice.
