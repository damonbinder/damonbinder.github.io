## Ingredients

* 500 g cooked skinless roast pork belly, chilled
* 200 g yoghurt
* 1 tsp ground mustard
* 30 g citron or lemon pulp, finely chopped
* Pinch of asafoetida

## Method

1. Slice the chilled pork belly into pieces about 3 mm thick and 5–7 cm long.
2. Whisk the yoghurt with the mustard, citron pulp, and asafoetida. Fold in the pork and rest for 20 minutes before serving.

## Notes

* Citron is hard to find; lemon pulp with a little finely grated peel is the closest substitute.
